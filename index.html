FAQ

Who is coaching for?

Mid-to-senior leaders in complex organisations who are navigating a bigger remit, working across teams or trying to make space for strategic decisions alongside delivery.

Is coaching therapy or mentoring?

No. Coaching focuses on future growth, self-awareness and action. Aman won’t tell you what to do; she helps you uncover what works for you.

Do you coach teams or just individuals?

Both. The primary focus is one-to-one executive coaching, with corporate coaching contracts and facilitated workshops for teams and organisations.

How do I know if we’ll work well together?

Chemistry matters. The free discovery call lets you experience the coaching style and decide whether it feels like the right fit.

How do I know whether coaching has worked?

Success is defined by your goals. You agree clear outcomes at the start and regularly review progress, confidence, behaviour and impact.

What if it doesn’t feel right?

Coaching is collaborative. If something isn’t working, you review it openly together. You are always in control of the process.

How is coaching different from the quiz?

The quiz reflects the habits you report over four weeks. Coaching gives time to explore the context, practise an approach and return to what happened.

Is the Snapshot a psychometric test?

No. It’s a coaching reflection tool, not a diagnosis, performance rating or validated assessment.

Where is my information stored?

Information is treated as confidential and stored securely in line with data protection law. Quiz answers stay in your browser unless you choose to save a report request. See the privacy notice.

The approach

How coaching works.

  1. Explore the fit

    Bring a real challenge to a complimentary discovery call. We’ll talk about what you want to change and see how it feels to work together.

  2. Define success and practise

    We agree what progress would look like. Between sessions, you try a different approach in the meetings, decisions and conversations that matter to you.

  3. Review and sustain

    We look at what changed, what still needs attention, and how you’ll keep practising when the pressure rises again.

Before we meet

A few questions.

Who is coaching for?

Mid-to-senior leaders navigating a bigger remit, working across teams, or trying to make space for strategic decisions alongside delivery.

What happens on a discovery call?

We discuss one challenge, what you’ve already tried and what you’d like to change. You can ask about the process before deciding whether to go further.

How is coaching different from the quiz?

The quiz reflects the habits you report over four weeks. Coaching gives us time to explore the context, practise an approach and return to what happened.

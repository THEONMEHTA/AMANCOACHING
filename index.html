Who is this for?

Mid-to-senior leaders and people taking on greater responsibility. The reflection covers everyday leadership habits across sectors.

What will my report tell me?

It reflects your own responses across four areas and suggests one place to focus. It is not a diagnosis, personality type or objective rating of your ability.

Is the quiz free?

Yes. You can view your report and download the PDF at no cost. A discovery conversation is optional.

What happens on a discovery call?

You can discuss your current challenge, ask about coaching and decide with Aman whether the approach fits. Submitting a request does not confirm a booking or commit you to coaching.

Will you email me afterwards?

If you share your details, Aman can follow up about your snapshot. Coaching tips and offers require a separate, optional opt-in. Your report is available here to download; it is not automatically emailed.

Can I enquire for my organisation?

Yes. Use the request form to ask about corporate coaching or workshops. You do not need to complete the individual quiz.

Accessibility

Privacy & your data

Last updated 18 November 2026

Aman Coaching uses information you choose to submit to respond to enquiries and follow up about your leadership snapshot. Email achieve@amancoaching.com to ask about access, correction, deletion or withdrawing marketing permission.

Quiz and report

Quiz answers are held in this browser tab while you complete the quiz. We do not save them in your browser’s local storage. Downloaded reports stay on your device. Closing or reloading the page clears your report.

If you submit your details

Your name, email, role, chosen focus, quiz answers, report version and marketing preference are submitted securely to Aman Coaching’s Netlify Forms account. Discovery requests include your message instead. Netlify processes submissions and spam checks as a service provider. Access is restricted through the site owner’s account.

Email choices

Coaching tips and offers are optional. Leaving the box unchecked will not prevent you getting your report. To withdraw permission, email achieve@amancoaching.com and say you would like to unsubscribe. This website does not automatically send a marketing sequence or email your report.

Retention and hosting

Enquiries and quiz submissions should be reviewed after 90 days and removed when no longer needed, unless you agree to ongoing coaching or communication. The site owner manages deletion from Netlify. Hosting services may retain technical request logs under their own policies. This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies.

Read Netlify’s privacy policy. Coaching reflection is not a clinical or psychological assessment. Do not submit health information or confidential details about other people.

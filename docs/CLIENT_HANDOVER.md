# Aman Coaching handover

## What is included

- A responsive launch page introducing leadership coaching and the free Leadership Snapshot.
- Six tailored questions covering role, preferred focus and four working habits.
- Four personalised report variants, balanced/tied response handling and three actions over 30 days.
- Immediate on-page results and a downloadable three-page PDF.
- Optional name/email capture, separate optional marketing permission and a discovery-call request form.
- Public business contact: **achieve@amancoaching.com**.
- Privacy information, accessibility page, local fonts, error page and deployment configuration.

## How enquiries reach you

Once deployed to Netlify with both email notifications enabled, quiz leads and discovery requests can arrive at **achieve@amancoaching.com**. They also remain available in the Netlify Forms dashboard. Receipt in the mailbox must be checked before launch. The website does not confirm a booked appointment; arrange the conversation directly or supply a calendar URL later.

## What visitors should know

The snapshot is a coaching reflection, not a validated assessment. Reports are generated immediately and are not automatically emailed. Visitors may view the report without submitting details. Reloading the page clears their answers and report; a downloaded PDF remains on their device. Marketing permission is optional and no automatic newsletter campaign is installed.

## Before handing over the live website

Confirm the deployed URL, both form notifications, portrait/copy/privacy approval and a controlled live lead test. See `DEPLOYMENT.md` for the exact steps. Store payment and delivery records separately from public website content.

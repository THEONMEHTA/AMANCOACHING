# Aman Coaching preview handover

## Included

- Responsive launch page introducing leadership coaching and the free Leadership Snapshot.
- Six tailored questions covering role, preferred focus and four working habits.
- Four personalised report variants, balanced/tied response handling and three actions over 30 days.
- Immediate on-page report and downloadable three-page PDF.
- Optional name/email capture, separate future marketing permission and a discovery-call request form.
- Private submission storage on Vercel, with no automatic email sending.
- Privacy information, accessibility page, local fonts, error page and deployment configuration.

## Review leads without email

In the authenticated Vercel project, open **Storage → aman-coaching-preview-leads → Browse → submissions**. Quiz leads and discovery requests are stored separately. See `DEPLOYMENT.md` for private JSON/CSV export instructions. Her mailbox credentials are not required.

The intended future business contact is **achieve@amancoaching.com**, supplied by the user. The preview does not notify that address or respondents. No marketing sequence or checkout is installed, and a saved enquiry does not confirm an appointment.

## Visitor expectations

The snapshot is a coaching reflection, not a validated assessment. Visitors can view and download their report without submitting personal details. Answers clear on reload; PDFs remain on their device. Preview wording explains that submitted details are stored privately for project review and no email is sent.

## Status and next steps

The live preview URL, tested source commit and verification status are recorded in `RELEASE_STATUS.md`. A preview is not an assertion that the client has accepted delivery or paid. Client approval, production account ownership, domain cutover and future notifications remain separate decisions.

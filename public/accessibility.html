Accessibility

The website supports keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators and reduced-motion preferences. Quiz answers use labelled radio buttons, and errors and submission states are announced through status messages.

Your leadership report

Your full report appears as HTML on this website. A PDF download is also available, but the PDF is not tagged for screen readers. Use the on-page report for the more accessible reading experience.

If something gets in your way

Email achieve@amancoaching.com with the page and a description of the problem. You can enquire about coaching without taking the quiz.

Your privacy.

Taking the Snapshot

Your fifteen answers and results stay in this browser tab unless you request a copy. Refreshing or closing the page clears the reflection. You can download a PDF to your own device without sharing details.

Requesting a copy or a call

A Snapshot request stores your email address, five area totals, your chosen focus, report version and optional marketing preference in private storage. We use those details to prepare the copy you requested. This preview saves requests privately and sends no emails.

For a discovery enquiry we store your name, email and any message you choose to include. Keep personal health information and confidential workplace details out of the form. Project administrators can review requests in the private Vercel store.

Measuring the funnel

We count quiz starts, completed steps, completions, result views, report requests and clicks to book. These counts exclude answers, scores, contact details and visitor identifiers. Hosting services also process technical information needed to serve the website.

Contact and deletion

To ask about your information, request deletion, or withdraw a marketing preference, email achieve@amancoaching.com. Opening an email link uses your own email app. A scheduling provider, when enabled, receives no quiz results from this website.
